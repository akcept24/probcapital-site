## What Is Prop Trading?

**Proprietary trading** (prop trading) = Trading a firm's capital after passing an evaluation challenge.

**How it works:**
1. Pay evaluation fee ($79-$1,299)
2. Pass challenge (hit profit target, respect rules)
3. Get funded account ($10k-$400k)
4. Trade and keep 80-90% of profits

**Top prop firms**: ProbCapital, FTMO, TopstepX

---

## What Is Traditional Trading?

**Traditional (retail) trading** = Trading your own money through a broker.

**How it works:**
1. Deposit capital ($10k-$100k+)
2. Trade any strategy
3. Keep 100% of profits (minus spreads/commissions)
4. Lose 100% of capital if you blow account

**Popular brokers**: Interactive Brokers, TD Ameritrade, IG

### 2. Risk Exposure

- **Worst case**: Lose fee, retake challenge

#### Traditional Trading:
- **Max loss**: Entire capital

- **Worst case**: Wipe out life savings

**Real statistics:**

**Winner**: Prop (much lower risk)

### 5. Rules & Freedom

#### Prop Trading Rules:
- ✅ Max drawdown (10-12%)
- ✅ Daily loss limit (5%)
- ✅ Minimum trading days (0-10)
- ✅ No overnight holds (some firms)
- ✅ No news trading (some firms)

**Pros**: Forces discipline  
**Cons**: Limits strategies (scalping, news trading)

#### Traditional Trading:
- ❌ No rules (except PDT in US)
- ✅ Trade any time, any strategy
- ✅ Hold positions forever
- ✅ Trade during news

**Winner**: Traditional (more freedom)
